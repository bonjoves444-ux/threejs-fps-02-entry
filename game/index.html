FERRYLINE

A round-based tactical FPS on a golden-hour ferry quay. Two teams of five fight over two charge sites — the Dockers hold the terminal, the Runners plant the charge at Site A (Ferry Berth) or Site B (Container Yard). Win rounds by elimination or by the objective, earn money, buy better guns. First to 7 rounds takes the match. Stopping to aim pays — spraying on the run does not. Headshots pay more.
